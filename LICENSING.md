# Licensing and publication boundaries

Every component has its own licensing decision. Public visibility, a download link or the name Community does not by itself grant an open-source software license.

No blanket software license is granted by this organization guidance. Follow the explicit license in the relevant repository or artifact and the applicable platform terms. Where no additional license is stated, do not assume broader reuse rights.

Specifications, documents, code, datasets and third-party materials may have different terms. A license must identify its actual scope.

The industrial compiler and selected commercial components remain proprietary. Unapproved manuscripts, confidential research, credentials and customer materials must not be published.

A prospective open-source component is published only after ownership, dependencies, redistribution rights and license scope are reviewed and explicitly approved. Contributions do not automatically transfer ownership.
